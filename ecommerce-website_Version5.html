About Our Nursery

Nurturing Uganda's Green Future

Green Grow Nursery and Garden Center started as a small local nursery with a passion for promoting green living in our communities. What began as a humble initiative has now expanded into an online platform to provide accessible gardening support to homes, schools, hotels, and organisations across Uganda.

We supply healthy, affordable plants and expert gardening practices to enhance the beauty of homes and environments. Our dedicated team provides personalised care guides, quality seedlings, and eco-friendly gardening solutions.

To become a leading and trusted provider of high-quality plants and gardening solutions, promoting environmental sustainability and greener communities.

To supply healthy, affordable plants and gardening practices, enhancing the beauty of homes and environments.

Care & Management Guides

Expert tips and techniques to help your plants thrive — from watering and sunlight to eco-friendly pest management.

Watering Guide

  • Water regularly but avoid over-watering; keep soil moist, not soggy.
  • Use watering cans or drip irrigation for efficiency.
  • Water in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation.

Sunlight Requirements

  • Match plants to their sunlight needs — Bougainvillea needs full sun.
  • Spathiphyllum (Peace Lily) prefers shade.
  • Provide shade for delicate seedlings and protect from heavy rain or wind.

Soil & Nutrition

  • Use fertile, well-drained soil mixed with compost or manure.
  • Repot plants as they grow larger to give roots room.
  • Mulch soil to retain moisture and reduce weeds.

Nursery Management

  • Ensure proper spacing between plants to prevent disease.
  • Weed regularly and label all plants clearly.
  • Prune plants like Duranta erecta to encourage healthy growth.

Plant Protection

  • Provide shade structures for delicate plants.
  • Protect plants from heavy rain and strong wind damage.
  • Use organic pesticides to manage pests safely.

Eco-Friendly Practices

  • Use organic pesticides and avoid harmful chemicals.
  • Promote recycling of pots and containers.
  • Recommend easy-care plants like Sansevieria to beginners.
